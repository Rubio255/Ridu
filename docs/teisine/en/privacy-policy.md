Version 2026-11-25. English translation; the Lithuanian version prevails. Draft: to be reviewed by a Lithuanian lawyer before publication.

## 2. What data we collect

From all users:
- name, email, phone number, login and security data (such as login time, device type, IP address);
- messages, offers and agreements in chats;
- reports of violations and complaints;
- chat filter flags: only the flag type and time, not the message text (see section 4c);
- referral programme data: who invited whom and whether the reward was granted;
- phone notification settings and the device notification token;
- reviews after a ride (rating 1–5 and a comment);
- your location if you tap “Alert Ridu with my location” (SOS) during a ride;
- plan and plan payment information (we do not store card details; Stripe handles them); for drivers, also the VAT number and address for the invoice if provided.

From clients:
- ride addresses, from which we derive the postal code, time and days;
- number and type of passengers (child, adult, senior), the child’s age or height if a seat is needed, and notes for the driver.

From drivers:
- location during a ride: from “Start trip” until the ride ends;
- the result and time of the identity and liveness check (see section “Identity and liveness check”);
- name, surname, business certificate number, LTSA permit number, vehicle registration number and the result of the check;
- photos of themselves and the vehicle.

We do not collect health data and do not store copies of documents, selfies or biometric data. Please write no more in notes than the ride needs.

## 4c. Chat filter

To protect against fraud, messages are automatically checked for requests for advance payment, bank or card details and invitations to continue off the platform. Phone numbers are not flagged. If such a sign is found, only a warning is shown and the message is still sent. We keep only the flag type and time, not the message text. If an account gets 3 flags within 7 days, an administrator is notified and decides. You may object to this processing by writing to info@ridu.lt.

## 4. Who sees your data

- Other users see only what is needed to agree. Until the client agrees to be contacted, the driver sees only the postal code, time and number of passengers. The driver sees the exact address only when the client shares it in the chat.
- Clients see the driver’s first name, approved photos and the verification badge. We do not show the permit number to clients.
- During a ride, the client sees the driver’s location. The client may share a link with another person (e.g. the other parent); it works only until the ride ends.
- Other users see reviews once both sides have rated or after 14 days. The first name, rating, comment and date are shown.
- A Ridu administrator sees an SOS alert with location. If needed, we pass it on to emergency services or the police.
- Plan payments: Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Ireland). We pass on only the email, name and internal account number, and at the driver’s request their VAT number and address for the invoice. Card details are entered and processed only by Stripe. Stripe also processes payment and fraud-prevention data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy).
- Service providers who help us: data storage and servers (Supabase, Vercel), driver identity and liveness checks (Didit), email delivery. They process data only on our instructions under contracts.
- Authorities, where the law requires (e.g. law enforcement, LTSA, the tax authority).

We store data in the European Union. If a service provider transfers data outside the EU, safeguards approved by the European Commission apply (e.g. standard contractual clauses).

## 4a. Identity and liveness check

We verify a driver’s identity through Didit (didit.me). At registration, the driver takes a photo of an identity document and of themselves. Later, for example every 7 days, before the first ride with a new client, at random or when we see a risk signal, Didit briefly checks that the same live person is at the phone.

Didit processes the image and the document as our data processor. Ridu does not receive or store them. We keep only the result (confirmed or not) and the time.

Comparing facial images is processing of biometric data, so we ask for your explicit consent before the check. You may withdraw consent. If you do not consent, your identity can be confirmed by a video call with Ridu.

After 2 failed attempts the profile is hidden until an administrator reviews the case. A person always makes the decision.

## 4b. Location during a ride and SOS

The driver’s location is processed only from the “Start trip” button until the ride ends. When the ride ends, the location is deleted and the sharing link stops working. We do not track location at other times.

The “Alert Ridu with my location” button sends your location to a Ridu administrator. It does not replace calling 112: in danger, call 112 first.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Account data: for as long as you have an account.
- Requests and chats: 12 months after the last message, so that disputes can be resolved.
- Exact addresses: while the request is active; afterwards only the postal code remains.
- Driver location during a ride: until the ride ends, then deleted.
- SOS alert with location: 30 days, or longer while needed for an investigation or dispute.
- Reviews: while the account of the user who wrote them exists.
- Identity and liveness check results and times: while the driver account exists; failed ones for 3 years.
- Data on violations, blocked accounts and fraud: 3 years.
- Accounting documents: 10 years, as the law requires.
- After you delete your account, data is deleted within 30 days, except data we must keep longer.

## 6. Children’s data

Children do not create accounts. A child’s data is provided by parents or other legal guardians, and only as much as a safe ride needs: age or height for the seat, first name for the handover. A child’s data is shown to the driver only after the client agrees to be contacted.

## 7. Your rights

You may:
- get a copy of your data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete data or your account;
- restrict or object to processing;
- receive data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent.

Write to info@ridu.lt. We reply within 1 month. If you think we have breached your rights, you may complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt).

## 8. Security

We use encrypted connections, access restrictions in the database, and two-factor login for drivers and administration. Staff see only what their work requires.

## 8a. Phone notifications

We send notifications only after you switch them on in the app and allow them on your device. You can turn off each type of notification in settings. The device token is deleted when you turn notifications off or delete your account.

## 9. Cookies

We use only cookies necessary for login and security. We would use statistics or advertising cookies only with your consent.
