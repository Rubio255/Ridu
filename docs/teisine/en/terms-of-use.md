# Ridu Terms of Use

Version 2026-11-25. English translation; the Lithuanian version prevails. Draft: to be reviewed by a Lithuanian lawyer before publication.

## 1. What Ridu is

Ridu is an online search platform. It helps people who need a ride for themselves, a child or a relative (clients) find drivers who hold an LTSA ride-hailing permit (drivers) and come to an agreement with them.

The platform is operated by MB „Įmonių veiklos analizė“, company code 307404233, registered office Giedraičių g. 39, R53, LT-09302 Vilnius, Lithuania, phone +370 649 94125, email info@ridu.lt (Ridu, we).

Ridu does not carry passengers, set prices, accept payments for rides or assign drivers. The ride contract is concluded directly between the client and the driver. Ridu is not a party to that contract and is not responsible for its performance.

## 2. Who may use Ridu

- Only adults (18 or older) with full legal capacity may use Ridu. Children do not create accounts: parents or other legal guardians book for them.
- One person may have only one client account and one driver account.
- When registering, you provide true data about yourself and keep it up to date.
- Only a person who holds a valid LTSA ride-hailing permit naming the vehicle, a driving licence and a registered business activity (for example, self-employment) may be a driver. Drivers deal with clients as traders.

## 3. Account and security

- Keep your login details safe and do not share them. You are responsible for actions in your account.
- If you think someone accessed your account without your knowledge, change your login at once and tell us.
- Ridu will never ask for your password, verification code or bank details by message or phone.

## 4. How search and agreement work

1. The client posts a request. Other users see only the postal code, time and number of passengers, not the exact address or name.
2. Drivers respond with a price. A driver may also publish a regular route.
3. When the client agrees to be contacted, a chat opens. The client shares the exact address when they choose.
4. The parties agree on price, time, payment and other terms themselves and conclude a contract. A contract template is available on the Pro plan.

Order of offers. Requests and offers are ordered by distance between postal codes, time and date posted. Requests and offers of Pro plan users may be shown higher. Such entries are marked “Boosted”. Ridu does not rate or rank drivers by how many rides they accept or decline.

## 5. What the “Trusted and verified driver” badge means

The badge is given to a driver whose LTSA ride-hailing permit and linked vehicle we checked against public LTSA data and whose photos we reviewed. The check is made at registration and periodically afterwards.

The badge does not guarantee that a ride will go without problems. Talk to the driver before concluding a contract, and meet them in the case of a child.

## 6. Client rights and obligations

The client has the right to:
- post requests free of charge within their plan, choose a driver or decline all offers;
- end a chat and block a user at any time;
- report a violation and receive a response.

The client must:
- give correct information about the ride and passengers;
- when booking for another adult, obtain their consent;
- when booking for a child, be the child’s parent or other legal guardian or have their consent;
- perform the contract concluded with the driver and pay the agreed price directly to the driver.

## 7. Driver rights and obligations

The driver has the right to:
- decide whether, when and how much to drive, what price to offer and which requests to skip;
- work through other platforms or independently at the same time;
- receive an explanation if their profile is restricted and appeal the decision.

The driver must:
- carry passengers only with a valid LTSA ride-hailing permit and only in the vehicle named in it, and update their data on Ridu the same day the permit expires or the vehicle changes;
- hold valid compulsory motor third-party liability insurance suitable for carrying passengers for payment, and keep the vehicle roadworthy with a valid technical inspection;
- carry children only in suitable child seats or boosters as required by the Road Traffic Rules;
- follow the Road Traffic Rules and rest requirements, and never drive tired or impaired;
- declare income and pay taxes themselves;
- give clients who are consumers all information required by law: name, surname, business certificate number, contact details, price and terms;
- use only their own photos and real photos of the vehicle;
- complete an identity and liveness check when Ridu asks (at registration, periodically, before the first ride with a new client, at random or when there is a risk signal). After 2 failed attempts the profile is hidden until an administrator reviews the case. The driver may ask to confirm identity by video call and may appeal the decision.

The driver is an independent carrier. The driver is not an employee or representative of Ridu and may not act on Ridu’s behalf.

## 8. Rides for children

- Before a child’s first ride, the client and the driver meet.
- A child is handed over only to the adult or institution named in the contract, unless the parents allowed in writing that the child may leave alone.
- The client states the child’s height and age so that a suitable seat is used.
- Ridu does not resolve disputes between parents. A parent who books for a child is responsible for having the right to do so.

## 9. Prohibited conduct

On the platform it is prohibited to:
- impersonate another person or use someone else’s or forged photos, permits or documents;
- carry passengers without a valid permit or in a vehicle other than the one named in the permit;
- ask for advance payment before the first meeting or ride, send payment links, or ask for codes or bank details;
- post fake requests or offers, collect other users’ data, copy it or use it for advertising;
- create several accounts, use a blocked person’s account or transfer an account to someone else;
- threaten, insult, harass, discriminate or behave violently;
- offer or request illegal services, such as carrying prohibited items;
- use bots or automated data collection, or attempt to breach system security;
- write fake reviews.

More tips are in “Safety and prohibited conduct”.

## 9a. Reviews

- A review may be written only after a ride that the parties agreed on through Ridu. This ensures that reviews come from people who actually travelled or drove.
- Both sides rate each other from 1 to 5 and may write a comment of up to 500 characters. Reviews are published once both sides have rated or after 14 days, so that no one can retaliate.
- Write honestly and about the ride. It is prohibited to write fake reviews, offer or ask for a reward for a review, threaten a bad review, or post other people’s personal data, insults or illegal content.
- Ridu does not edit reviews or order them by whether they are positive. We may hide a review that breaches these terms and explain why. You may contest an unfair review with the “Report” button.

## 10. Reporting violations

Report illegal content or conduct with the “Report” button in the chat or profile, or by email at info@ridu.lt. Say what the violation is, why, and where to find it.

We review reports without delay, urgent ones (concerning the safety of children or people) first. We tell the person who reported what we decided.

If someone’s life or safety is at risk, call 112. During a ride you may also tap “Alert Ridu with my location”. This button does not replace 112; Ridu is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee that the alert is reviewed at once. We inform law enforcement of such cases.

## 11. What Ridu may do about violations

Depending on how serious the violation is, we may:
- give a warning;
- hide or remove a request, offer, photo or message;
- temporarily restrict an account or its visibility;
- suspend an account while we clarify the circumstances;
- close an account and refuse a new one.

We explain every decision: what was decided, why, on what basis and how to appeal. The exception is where law enforcement asks us not to inform.

We close a driver account permanently with 30 days’ notice. We may act at once where the law requires it, where people’s safety is at risk, or in cases of fraud, forged documents, an invalid permit or repeated violations.

We may report possibly illegal activity, such as forged documents or fraud, to LTSA, the police or other authorities.
