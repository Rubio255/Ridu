# Safety and prohibited conduct

Version 2026-11-25. English translation; the Lithuanian version prevails.

## How to protect yourself from fraud

- Do not pay a stranger in advance before the first meeting or ride.
- Do not click payment links received in messages. Ridu charges no fee for rides.
- Never share your login code, password or bank details. Ridu never asks for them.
- Keep talking in the Ridu chat until you agree. That way the conversation history stays if a dispute arises.
- Check that the vehicle and driver match the photos. If they do not, do not get in and report it to us.
- Meet the driver before a child’s first ride.
- Make the contract in writing. Include the driver’s name, surname and business certificate number.

## What is prohibited

- Impersonating someone, using someone else’s photos or forged documents.
- Carrying passengers without a valid LTSA permit or in another vehicle.
- Asking for advance payment, codes or bank details.
- Posting fake requests, offers or reviews.
- Creating several accounts or using a blocked person’s account.
- Threatening, insulting, harassing or discriminating.
- Collecting other users’ data or using it for other purposes.

## What happens after a violation

Depending on the violation, we warn, hide content, restrict or close the account. In cases of fraud or forged documents we suspend the account at once and may inform the police or LTSA. We explain every decision, and you may appeal it.

## How to report

Tap “Report” in the chat or profile. If someone’s life or safety is at risk, call 112.
